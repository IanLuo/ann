The rhythm this implies

status (where are we) → run next (Ann proposes, builder approves) → the step runs → gate①/② (builder grills, confirms, or rejects with feedback) → repeat.
answer interrupts when the ladder needs the builder · change whenever reality shifts · check / resume / prune on maintenance.
Three shaping questions before the surface locks:
  1. Is the rhythm right — is run next the primary gesture (Ann proposes, builder approves, ≤1 interaction), or does the builder steer steps explicitly?
  2. Missing or wrong — anything a builder's hands reach for that's not here (project switch/open, undo/abort a step, search across the tree)?
  3. Config scope — F18 just provider + ask-vs-assume, or also step-chain templates (overlaps F3)?
